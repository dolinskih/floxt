# Privacy Policy for Floxt

**Last updated:** September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Floxt** desktop application developed by **Hubert Doliński** ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

---

### 1. Overview
Floxt is designed as a local-first, offline-capable note-taking application. We believe your notes and thoughts belong exclusively to you. The application does not track you, does not harvest personal information, and does not transmit your data over the internet.

### 2. Information We Do Not Collect
* **Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
* **Document Content:** The contents of your notes, documents, project files, and folder structures are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party service.
* **Telemetry and Analytics:** Floxt contains no analytics engines, trackers, crash reporting telemetry, or advertising frameworks.

### 3. Data Stored on Your Device
All data created or modified using Floxt remains strictly on your local machine:
* **Notes and Documents:** Saved exclusively in directories chosen by you using your operating system's native file picker.
* **Preferences and Settings:** Configuration details (such as theme choice, editor font size, line wrapping preferences, and UI states) are saved locally using standard browser storage mechanisms (`localStorage`).

### 4. System Permissions
Floxt requests access only to system features necessary for core editing functionality:
* **File System:** Floxt accesses your local storage strictly when you explicitly open, save, or import/export files or project folders.
* **External Links:** When you click an external link within a note, the application requests your default operating system browser to open that web address. Floxt does not track, log, or monitor external URLs clicked.

### 5. Third-Party Services
Floxt does not integrate with third-party tracking, advertising, or cloud analytics providers. 

### 6. Children’s Privacy
Floxt does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
